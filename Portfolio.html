Process

How an engagement runs.

Phase 1

Scope

We agree on targets, test windows and rules of engagement, and I get written authorization before anything is touched.

Phase 2

Recon

I map the attack surface: endpoints, login flows, dependencies and exposed services.

Phase 3

Controlled exploitation

I prove impact with careful, non-destructive tests. Nothing is broken and no data is kept.

Phase 4

Report

Findings are ranked by real risk, with reproduction steps and screenshots a developer can follow.

Phase 5

Fix and retest

I patch alongside your team, then retest until every finding is closed.
